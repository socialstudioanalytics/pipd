Project management software for PIPD

Twenty-six solutions screened for an organisation of roughly fifteen people, working in five languages across Ramallah, Belgium, Germany and Jordan, with no dedicated IT staff — and with a published commitment to BDS. The screen is applied first and the ranking second: a vendor that is a named boycott target, is an Israeli company, has an Israeli office, or runs on Microsoft Azure, Amazon AWS or Google Cloud is excluded before anything else is weighed.

The threat this is built around is not surveillance. It is administrative termination. In September 2025 OFAC designated Al-Haq, Al Mezan and PCHR under EO 14203, and Addameer three months earlier. Within weeks their Microsoft accounts were closed, Google services blocked, Mailchimp terminated without warning, three YouTube channels erased with more than 700 documented videos, and Addameer lost its cloud backup along with several months of its legal case database. No notice, no appeal, the whole estate at once.

Vendor origin dossiers

Verifiable corporate facts only — founders and nationality where publicly documented, country of incorporation, ownership and major shareholders, the company's own published office and R&D locations, and cloud sub-processor jurisdictions. Sources are linked on each card. The jurisdiction flags describe legal exposure — which state can compel disclosure — and are stated neutrally. They are not a judgment about any company or any individual.

Founder nationality appears because it was an explicit requirement of the brief. It is recorded only where publicly documented — inferred nationalities and birth countries reported second-hand have been removed, and where a founder's nationality is not on the record the field says so rather than guessing.

Reading the Israel and US fields. "No office found" means the vendor's own published location, affiliate and sub-processor lists contain no Israeli entity — it is the absence of evidence, not a positive assertion of absence. "Unverified" means a page the vendor publishes could not be retrieved (several return HTTP 403 or 404) and the question is genuinely open. Only positive findings are stated as confirmed.
